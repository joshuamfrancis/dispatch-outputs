# Travel Light for a 4-Day Official Visit

The goal: one carry-on bag, no checked luggage, nothing you won't use, nothing you'll wish you had.

---

## The Golden Rules

1. **One bag only.** A 40L carry-on (e.g. Away Carry-On, Osprey Farpoint 40) fits overhead and under the seat. No checked bag means no waiting, no lost luggage, and instant departures.
2. **Wear your bulkiest items on travel days.** Blazer, belt, dress shoes — wear them on the plane, don't pack them.
3. **Choose neutrals.** Black, navy, grey, and white mix freely. Every piece should pair with at least two others.
4. **Four days = three outfits.** You repeat one. Nobody notices and nobody cares.

---

## Clothing Checklist

### Tops
- [ ] 2 × dress shirts (white + light blue) — wrinkle-resistant fabric preferred
- [ ] 1 × polo or smart casual shirt (for less formal dinners)
- [ ] 3 × plain crew-neck undershirts (doubles as gym/evening wear)

### Bottoms
- [ ] 2 × dress trousers (charcoal + navy) — one worn on travel day
- [ ] 1 × dark jeans or chinos (for casual evening use)

### Outerwear
- [ ] 1 × blazer or suit jacket (worn on travel day, hung in hotel wardrobe)

### Shoes
- [ ] 1 × dress shoes (worn on travel day)
- [ ] 1 × clean white sneakers or smart loafers (casual/transit)

### Accessories
- [ ] 1 × belt (matches dress shoes)
- [ ] 1–2 × ties (if required — roll them, don't fold)
- [ ] 1 × pocket square (optional)
- [ ] 1 × watch

### Socks & Underwear
- [ ] 4 × underwear
- [ ] 4 × socks (2 dress, 2 casual)

---

## Toiletries (TSA/Airline 100ml Rule)

Pack everything in a single clear 1-litre zip-lock bag or a slim toiletry pouch.

- [ ] Travel toothbrush + toothpaste (travel size)
- [ ] Deodorant (solid stick preferred — no liquid rules)
- [ ] Facial cleanser (travel size or solid bar)
- [ ] Moisturiser / sunscreen (combined if possible)
- [ ] Razor + 2 blades (or electric shaver)
- [ ] Shaving gel (travel size) or solid shaving soap
- [ ] Lip balm
- [ ] Nail file / nail clippers (put in checked bag if flying internationally)
- [ ] Paracetamol / ibuprofen (strip pack)
- [ ] Antacid / Imodium (travel insurance for your stomach)
- [ ] Any prescription medication (carry-on always, never check)

> **Tip:** Most 4-star and above hotels provide shampoo, conditioner, and body wash. Skip these entirely.

---

## Tech & Documents

- [ ] Laptop + charger (universal adapter if international)
- [ ] Phone + cable
- [ ] Power bank (check airline mWh limit — typically 100Wh max)
- [ ] 1 × USB-C multi-port hub (replaces multiple adapters)
- [ ] Earbuds / ANC headphones
- [ ] Passport + photocopies (keep one copy in bag, email one to yourself)
- [ ] Visa / entry documents (printed and digital)
- [ ] Travel itinerary (printed — don't rely on battery for border control)
- [ ] Hotel booking confirmation
- [ ] Business cards (10–15 is enough)
- [ ] Expense receipts folder (slim accordion folder or envelope)

## Bag Organisation

```
Carry-On (40L)
├── Top compartment
│   ├── Toiletry pouch
│   └── Medications
├── Main compartment
│   ├── Packing cube 1: Clothing (shirts, trousers folded flat)
│   ├── Packing cube 2: Underwear & socks
│   └── Shoes (in a shoe bag, soles facing out)
├── Laptop sleeve / tech pouch
│   ├── Laptop
│   ├── Chargers, cables, hub
│   └── Power bank
└── Front pocket
    ├── Passport, documents, cards
    ├── Business cards
    └── Pen + notebook
```

**Packing cubes** are the single biggest upgrade for carry-on travel. They compress clothes, keep categories separate, and let you live out of the cube in the hotel without unpacking everything.

## 4-Day Outfit Rotation

| Day | Outfit | Notes |
|-----|--------|-------|
| Day 1 (Travel + Arrival) | Blazer + dress shirt + charcoal trousers + dress shoes | Wear all of this; freshen up on arrival |
| Day 2 (Official meetings) | Blazer + white shirt + navy trousers + dress shoes | Most formal combination |
| Day 3 (Meetings + dinner) | Navy trousers + polo/smart casual shirt + loafers | Drop the tie for dinner |
| Day 4 (Final meeting + departure) | Repeat Day 1 outfit | Freshly hang it overnight; it'll be fine |

## Hotel Habits That Save Space

- **Hang clothes immediately.** Wrinkles drop out of most dress fabrics overnight.
- **Use the iron.** Every hotel has one. 90 seconds on a collar is faster than carrying a separate shirt.
- **Hand-wash socks and undershirts** in the sink with hotel shampoo. They dry overnight on the towel rack.
- **Request extra hangers** from housekeeping on arrival.
- **Keep the safe empty.** Documents travel in your bag with you, not left in the room.

---

## What to Leave Behind

| Item | Why to skip it |
|------|---------------|
| Full-size shampoo / conditioner | Hotel provides it |
| More than 2 pairs of shoes | Weight, space, and you won't wear them |
| A full suit bag | Wear the blazer; fold the trousers flat in a cube |
| Books / physical reading | Phone or e-reader |
| Excessive adapters | One universal hub covers everything |
| Casual clothes for every evening | Repeat one outfit; no one tracks it |
| Gym gear (unless you will definitely use it) | Hotel gym towels are already there; pack one pair of shorts at most |

---

## Pre-Travel Checklist (Night Before)

- [ ] Charge all devices and power bank to 100%
- [ ] Print itinerary, hotel confirmation, and any visa documents
- [ ] Check airline baggage and liquid rules for your specific route
- [ ] Confirm carry-on dimensions comply with your airline
- [ ] Lay out travel day outfit (wear it — don't pack it)
- [ ] Set a phone alarm for your transfer / check-in time
- [ ] Download offline maps for your destination city

---

*Pack less than you think you need. You will not regret it.*
